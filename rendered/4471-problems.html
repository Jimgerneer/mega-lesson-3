parser: the shared parser reads every date month first

A day-first tenant books 7 June; the invite says 6 July. It hits every tenant and every channel (app, email and form). This is the bug #4471 names. Fixed on 5 October.

hint: booking-v3’s form tells people to type month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People do what the hint says, then the form reads their date the tenant’s way, day first. The parser fix does not touch this: the hint and the form disagree with each other. Only reports that came in through the form of a booking-v3 tenant (alder.physio, copper.studio, pale.harbour) belong here. The fix was the hint, not the parser. Engineering retired the booking-v3 form instead, which ends it for new bookings.

shift: dates move by one day

These reports are not a flip. “Tuesday 12 May, calendar says Monday 11 May”. “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday, client in Toronto got Monday”. “Every booking in June is one day off”. A day and a month that trade places cannot do that. It points at time zones or a daylight-saving boundary, not at date order. The desk saw it (desk-notes.json, “one-day move”) and still filed these under the parser, by tenant, so the parser fix closed them on paper. They are still open.

Why they were merged

  • Decision 0001 merges on subject line alone, and every one of these reports says some version of “wrong day”. Same words, different bugs.
  • Intake’s tag comes from the reporter’s browser, not from the tenant: every report tagged hint came from an en-US browser. Tag and cause disagree in both directions (see below). Do not triage by the tag.
